Pillar philosophy

How to think about this pillar

This pillar separates the reason for offering non-firm access from the technical condition that makes firm access unavailable, and identifies which customers may use the arrangement.

Design tutorial

Read the pillar as a design sequence

Start with the network need, then distinguish the system benefit, wider social value, eligible users and the technical connection criteria.

1. Identify the system-operator rationale

State whether the agreement avoids reinforcement, defers it, or provides an interim connection while committed reinforcement is delivered.

2. Separate benefits from technical causes

Do not confuse the objective of the agreement with the physical constraint that prevents firm access.

3. Define who is eligible

Record whether generation, demand, storage, new connections or capacity increases can receive the flexible offer.

Expert questionnaire

Evaluate this pillar

Does this pillar clearly distinguish why flexible access is offered, the grid condition that makes it necessary, and who is eligible to use it?

  1. Does the pillar clearly separate the SO's reason for offering a flexible connection (the benefit) from the technical cause that makes it necessary (the connection criteria)?
  2. After reviewing this pillar, is it clear why an SO would choose a Flexible Connection Agreement instead of reinforcement, local markets or other flexibility mechanisms and under what conditions?
  3. Are the network connection criteria (capacity, voltage level, short-circuit power, thermal limits, security criteria) clearly distinct from each other, or do some of them overlap?
  4. Is the distinction between new connections and capacity increases for existing users clear and useful in this pillar?
