## FCA Pillar 1 — Rationale and eligibility

**Opening question**  
Does this pillar clearly distinguish why flexible access is offered, the grid condition that makes it necessary, and who is eligible to use it?

**Please address:**

1. Does the pillar clearly separate the SO's reason for offering a flexible connection (the benefit) from the technical cause that makes it necessary (the connection criteria)?
2. After reviewing this pillar, is it clear why an SO would choose a Flexible Connection Agreement instead of reinforcement, local markets or other flexibility mechanisms and under what conditions?
3. Are the network connection criteria (capacity, voltage level, short-circuit power, thermal limits, security criteria) clearly distinct from each other, or do some of them overlap?
4. Is the distinction between new connections and capacity increases for existing users clear and useful in this pillar?

**Optional context requested from the reviewer**
- Country / jurisdiction
- Project, DSO or regulatory context (if shareable)
- Current terminology used for flexible / non-firm connections

## FCA Pillar 2 — Contractual conditions

**Opening question**  
Do these contractual dimensions make the duration, curtailment exposure and cost allocation of a flexible connection sufficiently clear for users and system operators?

**Please address:**

1. Do the options for pre-definition of curtailment (peak/off-peak, seasonality, none) cover how curtailment can be pre-defined?
2. Should maximum curtailment be considered a contractual condition or an operational characteristic of the connection?
3. Does the pillar make the difference between “Connection costs” and “Network charges” clear enough?
4. Do the curtailment allocation options (pro-rata, LIFO, auction, proportional to congestion created) reflect how curtailment is shared among users in practice?

**Optional context requested from the reviewer**
- Country / jurisdiction
- Project, DSO or regulatory context (if shareable)
- Current terminology used for flexible / non-firm connections

## FCA Pillar 3 — Curtailment activation and operation

**Opening question**  
Do the trigger, notice and activation dimensions together describe the full operational sequence of a flexible connection curtailment?

**Please address:**

1. Do event type triggering activation, curtailment notification and activation mode together describe the full sequence of a curtailment event (trigger, notice, execution) or is a step missing?
2. Do the event types (emergency, maintenance, congestion) cover all the situations in which curtailment can be triggered?
3. Is activation mode truly independent of notification time, given that automated schemes generally allow shorter notice periods?
4. Do you think the pillar can capture how curtailment is performed, either as a partial power limitation or as a full disconnection?

**Optional context requested from the reviewer**
- Country / jurisdiction
- Project, DSO or regulatory context (if shareable)
- Current terminology used for flexible / non-firm connections

## FCA Pillar 4 — Economic arrangements

**Opening question**  
Does this pillar make the allocation of curtailment value, market opportunity and financial responsibility sufficiently clear to prevent conflicting incentives?

**Please address:**

1. Are compensation mechanisms, value stacking opportunities and imbalance responsibility independent dimensions or should some of them be merged?
2. Does the pillar adequately capture how risks and rewards are distributed between the customer and the SO?
3. Does this pillar make it possible to identify situations where double charging or double compensation may occur?
4. Do the compensation options (fixed, set by the local flexibility market, local-market indexed, none) cover all possible ways of compensating curtailed users?

**Optional context requested from the reviewer**
- Country / jurisdiction
- Project, DSO or regulatory context (if shareable)
- Current terminology used for flexible / non-firm connections
