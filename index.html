Why we selected three mechanisms for deeper research

Three interfaces where regulation, system operation, and customer participation meet

We focus on three interfaces: price signals, access conditions, and explicit service procurement.

Price

Tariff design

Implicit and preventive. Useful for recurring network needs, but sensitive to granularity, fairness, response, and interaction with explicit markets.

Access

Flexible connection

Contractual and locational. It can unlock capacity quickly, but requires clear curtailment rights, compensation, control, and a reinforcement pathway.

Service

Local flexibility market

Explicit and measurable. It can reveal flexibility value, but depends on products, liquidity, clearing, coordination, settlement, and network constraints.

The research gap is often between the mechanisms

A customer may face a tariff, a connection condition, and a local-market opportunity at the same time. The key questions are signal priority, baselines, double commitment, compensation, liquidity, and reinforcement.
